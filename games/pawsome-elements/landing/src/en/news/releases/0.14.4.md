Hey everyone!
We’ve just dropped a small update — **v0.14.4**. Here’s what’s new:

## 🗺 Arenas & Card Backs

![Arenas & Card Backs](/public/pages/news/releases/0.14.4/0.14.4_1.webp)

You can now customize your matches with in-game arenas and card backs! Equip them via the
Atlas/Inventory menu and they’ll appear in-game. We’ve started with 3 arenas and 4 card
backs, with many more coming in the next updates.

## 🏆 Finish Screen Update

![Finish Screen Update](/public/pages/news/releases/0.14.4/0.14.4_2.webp)

The finish screen got a visual and functional upgrade — it's now cleaner, clearer, and more
satisfying.
