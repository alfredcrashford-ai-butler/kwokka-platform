Hey all!
We’ve just dropped a small but not small update — **v0.18.0**. Here’s what’s new:

## 🃏 Brand New Card Layout

![Brand New Card Layout](/public/pages/news/releases/0.18.0/0.18.0_1.webp)

![Brand New Card Layout in game](/public/pages/news/releases/0.18.0/0.18.0_2.webp)

There were many problems with the old layout of the game cards: it was unintuitive, the Element
was not descriptive enough, and the value of special or interactive cards was completely missing.
Another issue we received feedback on was that when players had many cards in hand, it was
challenging to find the right one. On top of that, the visuals left much to be desired.

That’s why we took the time to create the best kind of card for Pawsome Elements! Just look at this
beautiful thing—clear, stylish, and visually rich. We’re going to continue improving other aspects
of the cards, but this is a big step forward. The fantastic era of the new card layout
has just begun!

## 🖼️ Card set preview

![Card set preview](/public/pages/news/releases/0.18.0/0.18.0_3.webp)

We've added a new menu page - Card Set Preview! You can access it through your journal and check
out all the cards in the Standard set, examine their effects and visuals.

## 🐞 Bugfixes

We managed to locate and fix several irritating bugs that were affecting gameplay and app
functionality. We’ll continue working in this direction and focus on improving the app’s stability
over the next few weeks.
