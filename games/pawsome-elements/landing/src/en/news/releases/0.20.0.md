Hey everyone!
We’ve just dropped an update — **v0.20.0**. Here’s what’s new:

## 🃏 Golden Cards

![Golden Cards](/public/pages/news/releases/0.20.0/0.20.0_1.webp)

Unlock golden version of cards through Atlas/Journey page and see their spectacular
appearance in-game.

## ⏸️ Game Pause

We've added a pause to the game, which starts automatically whenever a player leaves a match. If
they reconnect within 10 seconds - the game will simply continue from where it stopped. But if
they leave for good - they will be removed from the game and it will continue without them.

## 🐞Bugfixes

We managed to locate and fix few small bugs that were affecting the game process.
