Hey everyone!
We’ve just dropped an update — **v0.24.2**. Here’s what’s new:

## 🐎 Essence Multiplier Track

![Essence Multiplier Track](/public/pages/news/releases/0.24.2/0.24.2_1.webp)

As per your feedbacks we moved Essence Multipliers to a separate track. You don't need
to search for it in the Spells or Journey sections anymore.

## 🏟️ New Arenas and Card Backs

![New Arenas and Card Backs](/public/pages/news/releases/0.24.2/0.24.2_2.webp)

We've added 2 more brand new arenas: BasenjiCo Lab and Ancient Library! Additionally, we've
updated the looks of the card backs and added 2 brand new ones to the collection.

## 🐞Bugfixes

We managed to locate and fix few small bugs that were affecting the game process.
