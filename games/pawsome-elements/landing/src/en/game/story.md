**A Lost Civilization, a Magical Catastrophe, and the Dogs Who Remain**

## A World Shattered by Magic

Long ago, the planet **Keta** was ruled by dogs—not as pets, but as the architects of a thriving
civilization. Their society advanced, orderly, governed by the **Ketan Canine Federation (KCF)**.

Then, in an instant, everything changed.

The **Enchanter Root** appeared without warning in the heart of **New-Tails**, pulsing with an
unknown energy. Authorities scrambled to contain it, but the moment the first dog touched it,
magic erupted into the world.

What followed was the **Shatter** — an event so catastrophic it split history into two eras:
**Before the Shatter (BGS)** and **After the Shatter (AGS)**. Governments collapsed. Cities fell.
The old world vanished, replaced by a new age where only the magical ruled.

Now, centuries later, whispers persist of those who shaped this fate:

- A **condemned outcast** who first wielded the **Enchanter Root's** power.
- A vanished, lost in time brother.
- A soldier erased from history, who returned unchanged to a world he no longer recognized.

Magic is the law. The past is a shadow. And somewhere, hidden in the ruins of the old world, lies
the truth behind the **Great Shatter** - and the key to breaking its cycle.

## Characters You May Encounter

Throughout your journey in _Pawsome Elements_, you may come across legendary figures from the
time of the **Great Shatter** — some guiding you, others challenging your path. Their presence
in the world of **Keta** is subtle, but their influence is everywhere.

### The First Touched

![The First Touched](/public/pages/game/story/ruffini.webp)

Some say he still walks the world, his presence woven into the fabric of magic itself. His symbols
mark hidden places; his teachings shape the factions that now rule.

### The Vanished

A name buried in old spellbooks and fragmented logs. His fate is unknown, but traces of his power
remain - if you know where to look.

### The Returned

![The Returned](/public/pages/game/story/paw.webp)

A figure out of time, erased in one age only to reappear in another. Some believe he seeks to undo
what the Shatter wrought. Others fear what that might cost.

### Sage Woofried

![Sage Woofried](/public/pages/game/story/woofried.webp)

Woofried appears early in your journey. He’s the one who explains the mechanics of spells,
the Elements, and the ancient history of Keta. But he may have more to
share — if **you prove you're ready**.
