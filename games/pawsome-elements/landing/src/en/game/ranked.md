## What is Ranked Mode?

![Screenshot of gameplay of Pawsome Elements game](/public/pages/game/ranked/02.webp)

Ranked Mode is a 4-player competitive format where players are matched based on skill and climb
a global leaderboard through smart plays, tactical spell use, and consistent performance.

Every ranked match impacts your position on the ladder — win, place, or fall. Unlike traditional
win-or-lose systems, Pawsome Elements uses a hard point system that rewards even second or third
place, making every round of the game impactful.

## How Ranked Matches Work

- **4 Players** per match
- **Turn-based card gameplay** with spells and interactive cards
- **Skill-based matchmaking** pairs you with similarly ranked opponents
- **One deck, one chance** – No pay-to-win, just pure strategy and quick thinking

Each game challenges your ability to adapt, read the table, and plan several moves ahead — all
while managing your hand, spells, and card timing.

## Rating & Progression

Our unique **hard rating system** is designed to make competitive play more nuanced and fair:

- 1st Place earns the most points, but
- 2nd and 3rd Place still make progress based on performance
- 4th Place loses points, but the drop is scaled to keep players encouraged to improve

This system rewards consistent smart play, not just occasional wins. You don’t have to dominate
every match — you just have to play well across time.

## Competitive Balance & Fair Play

- **No pay-to-win mechanics** – Ranked is fully skill-based
- **Cooldown-based spells** keep gameplay strategic and fair
- **Decks are standardized** – everyone plays on equal footing
- **Fast matchmaking** ensures quick entry into games, no long waits

Our commitment to competitive integrity means every player has a real shot — and every ranked
game is a true test of card game skill.

## Practice Makes Perfect

Before you jump into Ranked Mode, try:

- **Practice Mode** – Learn the rules and test spell combos against AI
- **Casual Games with Friends** – Experiment with new strategies in a low-pressure environment

Ranked Mode is always open, but the best players come prepared.
