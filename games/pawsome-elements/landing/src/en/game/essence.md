**Unlock Spells, Skins, and More Through Smart Gameplay**

In _Pawsome Elements_, every game you play earns you more than just bragging rights. You also
collect **Essence** — our core progression currency — used to unlock powerful spells and exclusive
cosmetic rewards.

Whether you win, place, or just play well, you’ll keep earning **Essence** and pushing your journey
forward. It’s our way of making sure every match matters.

## What Is Essence?

**Essence** is the **in-game currency** you earn by playing matches. It’s tied to **performance**
rather than just winning — meaning, the higher you rank in a single match, the more **Essence**
you receive.

Even if you don’t win, you can still rack up **Essence** through smart plays and strong turns.

## Essence Activation

Want to level up faster? As you play, you can unlock **multipliers** through **Essence Activation**.

You can also **double your Essence reward** at the end of each match by watching a short ad — an
optional boost for players who want to maximize their progress.

## What Can You Do With Essence?

**Essence** fuels your progress across two major areas in the **Atlas**, the central hub for
progression in _Pawsome Elements_.

### 1. Unlock Spells

Under the **Spells** tab in the Atlas, you can use **Essence** to unlock new spells. Each spell
you unlock adds more depth to your gameplay and more tools to your deck.

You can learn more about spells [here](/{{locale}}/{{links.game.spells}}).

### 2. Claim Rewards on the Journey

The **Journey** tab in the Atlas lets you exchange **Essence** for visual rewards, such as:

- **Card Skins** – Personalize your deck with new art styles.
- **Arena Skins** – Change the look and feel of your play space.
- **Profile Appearance** – Modify your profile with unique juicy styles.

These rewards don't affect gameplay — they're all about **style, expression, and flexing your achievements**.

Ready to earn more Essence? Jump into [Ranked Mode](/{{locale}}/{{links.game.ranked}}) for
competitive matches, explore all [Cards](/{{locale}}/{{links.game.cards}}) to improve your
game, or read the [Strategy Guide](/{{locale}}/{{links.game.strategy}}) to play smarter.
