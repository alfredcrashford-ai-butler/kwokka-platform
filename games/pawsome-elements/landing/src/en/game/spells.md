**Turn the Game Around with Strategic Magic**

In _Pawsome Elements_, every match is more than just cards and clever plays — it’s about knowing
**when to strike, when to adapt, and when to cast the perfect spell**.

Spells add a layer of strategy that separates the pups from the pros. You unlock them as you
progress and can equip a spell before each game. Instead of playing a card, you may choose to
activate your spell — but only once every few rounds, due to a cooldown. Use it wisely.

## What Are Spells?

![Spell usage in game](/public/pages/game/spells/02.webp)

Spells are **powerful abilities** that can bend the rules of the game, give you an edge, or
disrupt your opponents — and they don’t replace your cards, they **work alongside them**.

You can:

- Equip one spell before a match
- Use it **once every few turns**, instead of playing a card
- Turn the tide at critical moments

Some spells offer subtle advantages. Others can completely shift the board. All of them reward
timing, creativity, and bold moves.

## Featured Spells

Here’s a sneak peek at just a few of the spells you can unlock and equip:

### Pawgularity

Transform any card in your hand into a **Multidog** — the ultimate wildcard. Perfect for tight
situations where nothing fits.

### Hesitant Paw

Start with **two fewer cards** than everyone else. It’s risky, but gives you a serious head start
toward winning.

### Unleashed Will

Select any card in your hand and play it **without restrictions** — ignore the value or element.
Great for breaking out of a corner.

### Tailspin

Shuffle one random card from your hand back into the deck. Clean up your hand and hope the next
draw works in your favor.

### Fresh Scent

Tired of your current cards? Shuffle your entire hand into the deck and draw the same number of
fresh ones. Instant reset.

### Transmutation

Select one of your cards, then magically **transform one card from every opponent** into a copy
of that card. A bold move that can completely disrupt enemy plans.

## How to Unlock and Equip Spells

- Spells are unlocked by playing matches, gaining [essence](/{{locale}}/{{links.game.essence}}),
  and unlocking them in Atlas
- You can **equip your spell in the Atlas**.
- Practice with different spells in quick matches to find your style
- In ranked matches, the right spell at the right time can make all the difference

Whether you prefer disruption, risk-taking, or recovery, there’s a spell to match your playstyle.

## Why Spells Matter

- **Add strategic depth** without overcomplicating the game
- Encourage **long-term mastery** and creative problem-solving
- Create **clutch moments** that make every match memorable
- Keep gameplay dynamic and unpredictable
