**Everything You Need to Know About Pawsome Elements**

This page answers the most common questions from new and experienced players. Whether you are
learning the basics, troubleshooting, or curious about game mechanics — you will find the
answer here.

## Gameplay Questions

### How do I start playing?

Click the **Play Now** button on any page of this website. You will be taken to the game
application where you can register with a social account, email, or enter as a guest. After
that, you can start playing immediately.

### What are the game modes?

_Pawsome Elements_ offers three modes:

- **Practice** — Play against AI bots to learn the rules.
- **Quick Match** — Casual games with 2 to 6 players.
- **Ranked Match** — Competitive 4-player matches with a global rating system. Learn more
  on the [Ranked Mode page](/{{locale}}/{{links.game.ranked}}).

### How do cards work?

Each card has an **Element** (Nature, Filth, or Arcane) and a **Value** (1-9). On your turn,
play a card that matches either the Element or Value of the top card on the pile. If you
cannot play, draw a card from the deck. The first player to empty their hand wins.

See all card types on the [Cards page](/{{locale}}/{{links.game.cards}}).

### Can I play cards outside my turn?

Yes! If you hold a card that is an **exact match** (same Element and same Value) to the top
card on the pile, you can play it even when it is not your turn. This is a powerful tactic
that can disrupt the normal turn order.

### What is a Multidog?

The **Multidog** is a wildcard that can be played on any card, regardless of Element or Value.
Any card can also be played on top of it. Multidogs are rare and valuable — save them for
when they matter most.

### What does Ball of Fortune do?

When you play Ball of Fortune, you choose between **2 randomly selected effects** from a pool
of 5 possible outcomes: Ball of Curse, Ball of Fate, Ball of Luck, Ball of Wisdom, and
Ball of Wish. Each has a different effect on the game. Learn about each one on
the [Cards page](/{{locale}}/{{links.game.cards}}).

## Spell Questions

### How do spells work?

Before each match, you equip **one spell**. During the game, instead of playing a card on
your turn, you can activate your spell. After use, it goes on **cooldown** for several turns
before you can use it again.

### How many spells are there?

There are multiple spells you can unlock and use. Each has a different effect —
from transforming cards into Multidogs, to reshuffling your hand, to disrupting opponents.
See the featured spells on the [Spells page](/{{locale}}/{{links.game.spells}}).

### How do I unlock spells?

Earn [Essence](/{{locale}}/{{links.game.essence}}) by playing matches, then use Essence to
unlock spells in the **Atlas** — the game's central progression hub.

### Can I change my spell between matches?

Yes. You can equip a different spell before each match in the Atlas.

### How many cards do I start with?

Every player starts with **9 cards** drawn from the shared deck.

## Technical Questions

### What devices are supported?

_Pawsome Elements_ runs on any device with a modern web browser and internet connection.
Desktop and mobile devices are both supported. Chrome, Edge, Opera, and Safari are
recommended browsers.

### Can I play offline?

No. _Pawsome Elements_ requires an internet connection for all game modes.

### I am experiencing lag or crashes. What should I do?

Try the following:

- Clear your browser cache
- Close unnecessary browser tabs
- Update your browser to the latest version
- Restart your device

If issues persist, contact us via **contact@kwokka.co**.

### Is the game free?

Yes, 100% free to play and win. All in-game content can be unlocked by simply playing. There
are no pay-to-win mechanics. In-game purchases are completely optional.

### How can I turn ads off?

Ads are automatically disabled with your first in-game purchase.

### Can I create a private lobby for friends?

Yes, you can create a private lobby exclusively for your friends.

## Progression Questions

### What is Essence?

**Essence** is the in-game currency earned by playing matches. Your earnings are based on
performance — the higher you place in a match, the more Essence you receive. Even if you
do not win, you still earn Essence.

Learn more on the [Essence page](/{{locale}}/{{links.game.essence}}).

### What is the Atlas?

The **Atlas** is the central progression hub in _Pawsome Elements_. It has two main sections:

- **Spells** — Unlock new spells using Essence
- **Journey** — Exchange Essence for cosmetic rewards (card skins, arena skins, profile
  appearance)

### What are Essence Multipliers?

As you play, you can unlock **Essence Multipliers** — passive upgrades that increase the
amount of Essence you earn from each match. There are multiple tiers to unlock.

### What cosmetics can I unlock?

Through the Atlas Journey, you can unlock:

- **Card Skins** — Personalize your deck with new visual styles
- **Arena Skins** — Change the look of your play space
- **Profile Appearance** — Customize your profile with unique styles

These rewards are purely cosmetic and do not affect gameplay.

### Can I double my Essence rewards?

Yes, you can **double your Essence reward** at the end of each match by watching a short ad.
This is optional.

## Ranked Mode Questions

### How does the rating system work?

Ranked Mode uses a **hard rating system**:

- **1st place** earns the most rating
- **2nd and 3rd place** still gain rating
- **4th place** loses rating (scaled to keep it fair)

This rewards consistent smart play, not just occasional wins.

### Is ranked mode pay-to-win?

No. Ranked Mode is fully skill-based. Everyone draws from the same shared deck, and spells are
available to all players. There are no paid advantages.

### How many players are in a ranked match?

Ranked matches are always **4 players**.
