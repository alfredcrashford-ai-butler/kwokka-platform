**Who’s Top Dog This Season?**

Welcome to the _Pawsome Elements_ Global Leaderboard — the official rankings of the most
competitive pups in the world. This is where the fiercest spell-slingers and sharpest card
players climb their way to glory in Ranked Mode.

The leaderboard updates live throughout the season. Check back regularly to see who's rising,
who's slipping, and how close you are to the top. [Check out the leaderboard in-game]({{links.app}}).

## How Rating Points Work

Ranked matches use a **hard rating system**:

- 1st place earns the most points
- 2nd and 3rd still make progress
- 4th place loses points

Your rank reflects not just wins, but **consistent smart play**.

Learn more about Ranked Mode and how to climb the ladder on the [Ranked Mode page](/{{locale}}/{{links.game.ranked}}).
